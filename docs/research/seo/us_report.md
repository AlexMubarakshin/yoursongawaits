# US keyword demand — AI personalized song gifts (Ahrefs Matching terms, US)

> **Детальный отчёт (приложение).** Канонические цифры и решения — в `docs/research/facts.md` и `docs/research/05-decisions.md`; при расхождении верны они. Последняя сверка: 2026-11-05.

Source: `seo/raw/us.tsv` (UTF-8 copy of `seo/raw/google_us_ai-birthday-song-ai_matching-terms_2026-11-05_19-02-51.csv`), 8,601 rows, all country=us. SV trend window Nov-2024 → Oct-2026. Generated by `seo/scripts/us_analysis.py`.

## Key conclusions (US)

- **Gift-intent demand is small but growing fast.** GIFT_CORE+BIRTHDAY_NAME+OCCASION_*+RECIPIENT ≈ 16.4k/mo (14.7k excl. one anomalous query); explicitly custom/personalized/AI wording ≈ 10k/mo. Like-for-like 12-mo growth +23%; GIFT_CORE alone +98% LFL (custom song gift +71%, personalized song gift +89%, custom song +50%).
- **The money terms are hard; the birthday/occasion tail is easy.** GIFT_CORE median KD 52 (custom song gift KD 68, CPC $3.50; personalized song gift KD 64, $4.50) — Songfinch owns the SERP. BIRTHDAY_NAME and OCCASION_* have median KD 0–1 but CPC <$1 (mostly free/listen intent).
- **AI_GENERATOR is 73k/mo (72% of relevant volume) but is a tool market, KD 73, CPC $0.79, declining −10%.** Not gift demand; only the low-KD "best (free) ai song generator" listicle terms are attackable.
- **Branded: Songfinch ~4.6k/mo (−17% YoY), Songlorious ~1.7k/mo (+20%), Udio/Mozart/Mureka small but growing; GiftSong, CustomSong, MakeCustomSongs, Birthdai, Wondera have no US rows.** Songfinch decline + rising generic custom-song queries suggests demand is shifting from the incumbent brand to generic/AI-priced alternatives.
- **Seasonality:** Mother's Day (May) and Father's Day (June) are ~9–10× their monthly average; anniversary/wedding/recipient peak Dec–Feb (Valentine window); BIRTHDAY_NAME is flat (peak/trough 1.6×) — the evergreen base.
- **Programmatic names:** this export has essentially no `happy birthday <name> song` rows (seed artifact); the "… song with name" template is ~2.4k/mo at KD 0–12. Needs a dedicated name-seeded export to size.

## 0. Data notes & caveats

- Total Ahrefs volume across all 8,601 rows: **116,060/mo**. Only **1,511 rows (18%) have a non-empty volume**; the rest are long-tail (<10/mo, blank in export) and are counted as 0 in volume sums but their SV-trend arrays are still used for growth/seasonality.
- One head term dominates: `ai song generator` (52,000) is ~45% of all volume — always read AI_GENERATOR separately from gift-intent clusters.
- 1230 of 1511 rows with volume have **no KD** (Ahrefs has not computed it); they are excluded from KD medians and from the "low-KD" list (listed separately).
- Ahrefs volumes are rounded modelled estimates (buckets 10/20/…/100/150/…); sums of many long-tail rows overstate precision. Growth figures use the SV-trend arrays (sum of last 12 months vs prior 12), which differ from Ahrefs' own "Growth (12mo)" column.
- Traffic potential (TP) is a *parent-topic* metric: many keywords share one parent and repeat the same TP. TP is therefore **deduplicated by Parent Keyword within each cluster** (max TP per parent; rows without a parent use their own TP). Parents often point outside the niche (e.g. `songfinch` → parent "how to create a personalized song gift"; `custom song gift` → parent `songfinch`), so TP is a ceiling of what the best page could capture, not additive demand.

## 1. Cleaning rules (NOISE)

Applied in order; first match wins. Keywords are lower-cased.

| Rule | Definition | Rows | Volume |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| N1 | famous song/artist/game/franchise/non-personal anniversary | 579 | 1,060 |
| N2 | platform how-to (add custom song to Spotify/IG/alarm, game song ids) | 30 | 110 |
| N3 | listening/lyrics/regional-language/church/dance intent | 1,262 | 5,300 |
| N4 | media/download/reels/"latest" consumption without personalization | 682 | 890 |
| N5 | AI tool off-target (cover/voice clone/lyrics-only/title/video/parody/how-to) | 557 | 7,320 |
| N6 | unmatched / no gift, occasion, recipient or creation signal | 57 | 10 |

Details:

- **N1** famous songs/artists/franchises/games: Flintstones, Giveon "Heartbreak Anniversary", Al Jolson, Cowboy Junkies, Beat Saber/FNF/Roblox/Guitar Hero custom songs, Song of Achilles, Hannah Montana, Genshin, BTS, etc., plus work/company/death anniversaries.
- **N2** platform how-tos: "add custom song to Spotify/Instagram/alarm", "roblox custom song ids".
- **N3** listening/consumption: lyrics (except "lyrics to song / custom lyrics" which is creation intent), karaoke, chords, ringtone, instrumental, regional-language terms (hindi, tamil, punjabi, kannada, spanish, korean…), church/LDS/worship, walk-up, dance, DJ.
- **N4** media/download/reels/"latest"/"new 2025"/youtube/status/capcut unless a personalization signal is present ("with name", "custom", "personalized"). So `happy birthday song download with name` stays (BIRTHDAY_NAME) but `anniversary song mp3 download` is noise.
- **N5** AI tools that are off-product: covers, voice cloning / "with my voice", celebrity, parody, mashup, vocaloid, lyrics-only generators, song name/title generators, video generators, Suno/Udio UI how-tos — unless an occasion/custom word is present.
- **N6** catch-all: no gift, occasion, recipient, brand or creation signal (e.g. bare `anniversary song`-style variants already caught above, misc).

Note: plain `happy anniversary song` / `mothers day song` / `fathers day song` (listening intent but occasion-specific) are **kept** in their OCCASION cluster and flagged by a `gift_intent` column; per-cluster tables below show both all-intent and gift-intent volume.

Top noise keywords by volume: `ai song lyrics generator` (2200, N3), `ai cover song generator` (1300, N5), `ai song cover generator` (1000, N5), `ai song cover generator free` (500, N5), `ai song generator with voice` (300, N5), `ai voice song generator` (300, N5), `ai song lyric generator` (300, N3), `anniversary song latest` (200, N4), `ai song voice generator` (200, N5), `ai song generator with my voice` (200, N5), `best ai voice song generator` (150, N5), `free ai song generator with my voice` (150, N5), `flintstones happy anniversary song` (150, N1), `ai song cover generator free no sign up` (150, N5), `ai parody song generator` (150, N5)
