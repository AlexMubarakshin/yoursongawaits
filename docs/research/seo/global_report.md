# Global demand analysis — AI personalized song gift

Source: Ahrefs "Matching terms" worldwide export (115,755 keyword×country rows, 13,586 distinct keywords, 171 countries with non-noise volume). Volumes are monthly Google search volume per country. CPC in USD. Generated by `classify.py` / `analyze.py` / `report.py` in `seo/scripts/` (raw data: `seo/raw/global.tsv`; original Ahrefs CSV in `seo/raw/`).

## 0. Headline

- Total volume in export: **440,200**/mo. Non-English-language keywords: **23,770 (5.4%)**, of which Russian = 21,940. Gift-intent volume in a non-English language: **950** of 79,670 (1.2%) — essentially only Hinglish.
- The export was seeded with English terms (ai song generator, custom song, songfinch, anniversary song, happy birthday song with name, mothers/fathers day song) plus one Russian seed family ("создать песню нейросеть"). It therefore measures **where English queries are typed**, not native-language demand. Spanish/German/French/Portuguese/Italian/Turkish/Indonesian gift-song queries are effectively absent (< 0.1% of volume), which is an artefact of the seeds, not evidence of no demand.

## 1. Cleaning rule (explicit)

Rules are applied per keyword in this order (first match wins), see `classify.py`:
1. **NOISE – famous/off-topic**: artist/franchise/song titles (Kamen Rider, HK flag raising, Tony! Toni! Toné!, Abochi, Steel Panther, Bluey, Sidhu Moose Wala AI voice, …), chords/guitar/piano/tabs, school excerpts, Roblox/Spotdl/"Ultimate Custom Night" etc.
2. **NOISE – unrelated tool**: AI cover / voice clone / "with my voice" / remix / mashup / song→video / video generator / downloader / apk / song-name or title generator / company jobs, login, revenue.
3. **NOISE – physical merch**: plaque, wall art, canvas, print, music box, Spotify plaque, cards, vinyl, blanket (adjacent "custom song" merch, 4,060/mo, reported separately).
4. **NOISE – listening intent**: occasion/birthday/recipient queries containing mp3, download, status, capcut/template, lyrics, karaoke, ringtone, audio/video, old/new/latest/list, best/good/top/romantic, kids/kindergarten, church/christian/LDS/worship, Bollywood, or a language name (hindi, kannada, tagalog…) **and no personalization marker**. Personalization markers = with name / name / custom / personalized / AI / create / make / maker / generator / gift / write / order / photo (+ RU/ES/PT/DE/FR/IT/TR equivalents). So "happy birthday song download with name" stays (BIRTHDAY_NAME) but "anniversary song hindi" or "best song for anniversary" is NOISE. Birthday queries with no personalization are NOISE (generic happy-birthday listening).
5. Then: BRAND_COMPETITOR (Songfinch, Songlorious, ForeverSongs, GiftSong, RU clones…) → birthday (+person name → NAMES_PROGRAMMATIC, +personalization → BIRTHDAY_NAME) → OCCASION_* (multilingual patterns) → GIFT_CORE → RECIPIENT → AI_GENERATOR (generic AI song tools incl. Suno/Udio brand queries and RU "нейросеть") → OTHER_RELEVANT.
6. Occasion-specific AI queries ("ai birthday song generator", "ai christmas song generator") are counted in the gift clusters, not AI_GENERATOR. Plain "anniversary song"/"mothers day song" stay in OCCASION_* but are flagged *non-explicit*; the column `gift_strict_vol` keeps only gift rows with an explicit personalization marker.

## 9. Caveats

- **Seed bias.** The seeds were English, plus one Russian AI-tool family. Country volumes outside English-speaking markets therefore mostly count English queries typed abroad ("ai song generator" in DE/NL/NO). They say nothing reliable about native-language gift demand.
- **"Anniversary song" and "mothers day song" are mostly listening intent.** People want an existing song to play or post, not a custom one. The listening variants are NOISE (§1). The plain head terms stay in OCCASION_* but are non-explicit, so prefer `gift_strict_vol` when estimating buyers. India's 48.6k gift volume is 52% strict and is dominated by free "happy birthday song with name mp3" intent, at CPC ≈ $0.01–0.04.
- **CPC is thin.** Many non-English rows have no CPC, and Russia has none at all. A gift CPC that rests on one or two rows can be unrepresentative (ID $0.32). AI-generator CPC is the steadier purchasing-power proxy.
- **KD is missing** for most low-volume and non-English rows. A median KD of 0 mostly reflects low-competition long tail, not a measured lack of competition.
- **Growth.** Growth compares the last 12 months with the prior 12 in the SV trend. For small countries it is noisy, e.g. RS +775% from a 60/mo base.
- **AI_GENERATOR (301k/mo, 68%)** is excluded from relevant volume as asked. It is mostly tool-seekers, but it is the channel the AI competitors use for their machine-translated "birthday song with name" pages.
- **Physical merch is excluded.** "Custom song" merch such as music boxes, Spotify plaques and lyric wall art (4,060/mo) is NOISE here. It is an adjacent upsell or affiliate niche.
