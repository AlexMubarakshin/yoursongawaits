FAQ

Questions

Is the song made with AI?

Yes. AI writes the lyrics from your notes and sings them. You can edit any section, or ask for a rewrite, before the music is made.

What if the name sounds wrong?

You hear it before you pay. Tap Fix it, write how the name is pronounced, and we sing it again. On the gift page it's still spelled the normal way.

What's in the free preview?

The first 30–45 seconds of the same song you'd get, with the name in it. The full song unlocks after payment.

How does it get to them on the day?

Pick a date and time in their time zone, up to a year ahead. We email them between 08:00 and 21:00 their time, or remind you to send the link yourself.

Who sees the reaction video?

Only you, or the group named on screen. They agree before the camera turns on and again before it's sent. Videos are kept for 12 months and can be deleted.

What if I don't like it?

After paying you get 2 free regenerations. If it's still not right, you get a full refund within 14 days, as long as the gift hasn't been opened.
