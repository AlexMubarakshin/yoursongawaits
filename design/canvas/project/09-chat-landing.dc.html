Questions people ask

Something else? Ask in the chat

Is it made with AI?
Yes. The lyrics and the music are made with AI from your story, and you can edit any section of the lyrics before the music is made
What if the name sounds wrong?
You hear the name sung before you pay. Tap Fix it, spell it the way it's said, and you get an extra try for that
Can I hear the whole song before paying?
You hear the first 30–45 seconds of the same song you'll get, with the name in it. The full song unlocks after payment
Who sees the reaction video?
Only you. They're asked before the camera turns on, and again before the video is sent. They can delete it instead
Can I pick the exact time it arrives?
Yes, any date up to a year ahead, in their time zone. You can change it until the day
What if I don't like the result?
You get two free remakes after paying. If it still isn't right, we refund you in full within 14 days, as long as the gift hasn't been opened
