Notes

Questions, answered plainly

Do I pay before I hear it?

No. The first 30–45 seconds, with their name, are free. No account is needed until you buy

What if the name is sung wrong?

You check it before paying. Choose "Fix it" and spell the name the way you say it

Can I change the lyrics?

Edit any section, or ask for a rewrite in your own words, up to 10 times before the music is made

When will it arrive?

At the date and hour you choose in their time zone, up to a year ahead. The gift page stays open before then, showing when it opens

Who sees the reaction film?

Only you. They are asked first, it leaves their phone only if they send it, and they can open the gift without filming

Is it made with AI?

Yes, from your story. If it isn't right after paying, there are two free remakes, then a full refund within 14 days while the gift is unopened
