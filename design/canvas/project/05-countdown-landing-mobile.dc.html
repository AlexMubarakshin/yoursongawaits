Questions, answered

Is the song made with AI?Yes. AI writes the lyrics from your story and sings them. You can edit any section or ask for a rewrite before the music is made.
What if her name sounds wrong?You hear the name before you pay. Tap Fix it, write how it's pronounced, and we sing it again. It's still spelled correctly on her gift page.
What's in the free preview?The first 30–45 seconds of the same song you'd buy, with her name in it. The full song unlocks after payment.
How does it get to her on time?Pick a date and time in her time zone, up to a year ahead. We email her between 08:00 and 21:00 her time, or remind you to send the link yourself.
Who can see her reaction video?Only you. She agrees before the camera turns on and again before sending. Videos are kept for 12 months, and you can delete them any time.
What if I don't like the result?After paying you get 2 free regenerations. If it's still not right, you get a full refund within 14 days, as long as the gift hasn't been opened.
