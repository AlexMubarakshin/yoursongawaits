Liner notes

Questions people ask
What do I hear before I payThe first 30–45 seconds of the same song you'd get, with their name in it. You can ask for a second version, and try up to three times before paying.
What if the name sounds wrongTap "Fix it" and spell it the way it is said, like "Ah-na". The lyrics on the gift page keep the normal spelling.
How does it reach themWe email the gift link at the date and time you pick, in their time zone, up to a year ahead. Or you send it yourself by WhatsApp, iMessage or email, and we remind you when it's time.
Who sees the reaction videoOnly you, or the people in a group gift. Recording is their choice, and they can delete it.
Is it made with AIYes. The lyrics and music are made with AI from your story, and you can edit every line before the music is made.
What if I don't like the full songWe remake it up to two times for free. If it still isn't right, you get a full refund within 14 days, as long as the gift hasn't been opened.
