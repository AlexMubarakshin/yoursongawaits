Updated 7 Nov 2026 · 4 min read · by the openyoursong team

What changes the price

Who makes it is the biggest factor: a person or AI. Then come extras like rush delivery, more verses, a video or vinyl. The cheapest way to avoid paying for something you don’t like is a service that lets you hear the song before you pay

Questions

Why do some custom songs cost $200

A real musician writes and records each one, which takes days. AI songs cost less because the music is generated from your story in minutes

Are there extra costs

With us, no. The $29 includes the full song, the gift page, delivery on your date and the PDF card. The $55 bundle adds a second version, a photo slideshow and a lyrics poster

Can I get my money back

You get two free remakes. If it still isn’t right, you get a full refund within 14 days of paying, as long as the gift hasn’t been opened
