Questions about custom songs

How much does a custom song cost

$29 for the song with their name in it. $55 for the bundle, which adds a second version, a photo slideshow and a lyrics poster. You pay only after you’ve heard the free preview.

What do I hear before I pay

The first 30–45 seconds of the same song you'll get, with their name in it. You can try another version and fix how the name is sung before paying.

Is it made with AI

Yes. The lyrics and music are made with AI from your story, and you can edit every line. The gift page says so too.

Who sees the reaction video

Only you, or the people in a group gift. They are asked before the camera turns on and again before anything is sent. They can also skip it and just open the gift.

Can I get a refund

You get two free re-makes. If it still isn't right, you get a full refund within 14 days, as long as the gift hasn't been opened.
