Questions people ask

Something else? Write to us any time
Do I pay before I hear it?

No. You hear the first 30–45 seconds free, with their name in it. You don't need an account until you decide to buy

What if their name sounds wrong?

You hear how the name is sung before you pay. Tap "Fix it" and spell it the way your family says it

Can I change the words?

Yes. Edit any part of the lyrics, or ask for a rewrite, like "make it funnier" or "add the dog", up to 10 times before the music is made

When does it arrive?

On the date and time you choose, in their time zone, up to a year ahead. We can email it to them, or you send the link yourself

Who sees the reaction video?

Only you. They're asked before the camera turns on, and the video is sent only if they press send. They can also skip it and just listen

Is it made with AI? And what if I don't like it?

Yes, the music and voice are made with AI from your story. After paying you get two free remakes, then a full refund within 14 days if the gift hasn't been opened
