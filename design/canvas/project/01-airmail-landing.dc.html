Questions people ask

What do I hear before I pay

The first 30–45 seconds of the same song you'd get, with their name in it. You can ask for a second version, and try again up to three times before paying.

What if the name sounds wrong

Tap "Fix it" and spell it the way it is said, like "Ah-na". The lyrics still show the normal spelling.

How does it reach them

We email the gift link at the date and time you pick, in their time zone, up to a year ahead. Or you send the link yourself by WhatsApp, iMessage or email, and we remind you when it's time.

Who sees the reaction video

Only you, or the people in a group gift. Recording is their choice, and they can delete it.

Is it made with AI

Yes. The lyrics and music are made with AI from your story, and you can edit every line before the music is made.

What if I don't like the full song

We remake it up to two times for free. If it still isn't right, you get a full refund within 14 days, as long as the gift hasn't been opened.
