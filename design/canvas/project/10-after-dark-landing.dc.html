Before the show

Is it made with AI?

Yes. The lyrics and music are made with AI from your story. You can edit every section of the lyrics, or ask for a rewrite, before the music is made

What if their name is sung wrong?

You hear the name before you pay. Choose Fix it, write how it sounds, and you get an extra try for that

Do I hear the whole song before paying?

You hear the first 30–45 seconds of the same song, with their name in it. The full song unlocks after payment

Who sees the reaction video?

Only you. They agree before the camera starts and again before sending, and they can delete it instead

How far ahead can I schedule it?

Up to a year, to the minute, in their time zone. You can change the date until the day

What if I don't like it?

After paying you get two free remakes. If it still isn't right, you get a full refund within 14 days, as long as the gift hasn't been opened
