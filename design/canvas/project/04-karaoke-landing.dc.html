Before you take the mic

What's in the free preview

The first 30–45 seconds of the same song you'll get, with their name sung. You can hear a second version before you pay.

What if the name sounds wrong

Type it the way it sounds, for example "AH-na", and we sing it again. The name fix doesn't count against your tries.

Is it made with AI

Yes, the words and music are made with AI from your story, and you can edit every line. We say so on the gift page too.

Can they hear it before the day

No. Until the date and time you set, the link shows a waiting screen with who it's from and when it opens.

Who sees the reaction video

Only you, or the people in a group gift. They agree before the camera starts and again before sending. Nothing leaves their phone until then.

What if I don't like it

You get two free re-makes. After that, a full refund within 14 days, as long as the gift hasn't been opened.
